Privacy Policy

pause — Smoking Cessation Tracker

Effective Date: August 3, 2026

Quick Summary: pause respects your privacy. All your personal data stays on your device. We only collect anonymous usage analytics to improve the app experience. We never sell, share, or monetize your personal information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to pause ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use the pause mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All personal data is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to share it.

We store the following locally:

  • Smoking Logs: Date, time, intensity, and trigger tags for each cigarette logged
  • App Settings: Theme preferences, notification settings, daily limits, currency, time format
  • Wave Attempts: Records of your attempts to resist smoking cravings
  • Trigger Tags: Custom triggers you create to identify smoking patterns
  • Progress Photos: Optional photos you take to track your journey
  • App Preferences: UI customization, watch style, and other personal settings
Important: This data is stored entirely on your device. We cannot access, retrieve, or view this data. If you clear your browser data or uninstall the App, this information will be permanently deleted.

2.2 Anonymous Analytics Data

We use PostHog, a privacy-focused analytics platform, to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how the App is used and where we can improve.

Analytics data includes:

  • Feature usage patterns (e.g., which features you use most)
  • App performance metrics (load times, errors)
  • Session duration and navigation patterns
  • Anonymous device information (device type, OS version, screen size)

What we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email, or any personal identifiers
  • Your smoking logs or personal health data
  • Location data
  • Photos or media from your device
  • Contacts or social network information
  • Payment or financial information

2.3 Session Recordings

We use PostHog's session recording feature to understand user interactions. These recordings:

  • Mask all text input fields (no personal data visible)
  • Capture only UI interactions (taps, scrolls, navigation)
  • Are used solely for improving user experience
  • Are stored securely on PostHog's servers
  • Are automatically deleted after 30 days

3. How We Use Information

3.1 Local Data

Your locally stored data is used solely to:

  • Provide app functionality (tracking, statistics, reminders)
  • Personalize your experience (themes, settings)
  • Enable offline functionality

3.2 Analytics Data

Anonymous analytics help us to:

  • Identify and fix bugs or performance issues
  • Understand which features are most valuable
  • Make data-driven decisions for future updates
  • Improve overall app stability and user experience

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Share your data with third parties for marketing
  • Use your data for advertising purposes
  • Transfer your data to external servers (except anonymous analytics)

We may share data only when:

  • Required by law or legal process
  • Necessary to protect our rights or safety
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with prior notice)

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures:

  • Local Data: Stored in your browser's secure localStorage
  • Analytics: Transmitted via encrypted HTTPS connections
  • PostHog: SOC 2 Type II compliant platform
  • No Server Storage: We don't operate servers that store your data

6. Data Retention

  • Local Data: Retained until you delete it or uninstall the App
  • Analytics Data: Automatically deleted after 30 days
  • Session Recordings: Automatically deleted after 30 days
