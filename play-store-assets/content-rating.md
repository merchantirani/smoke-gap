# Content Rating Questionnaire - pause app

## IARC (International Age Rating Coalition) Questions

### 1. Violence
**Q: Does the app contain violence?**
**A: No**
- No depictions of violence
- No violent language
- No blood or gore

### 2. Sexual Content
**Q: Does the app contain sexual content?**
**A: No**
- No sexual imagery
- No sexual language
- No dating features

### 3. Language
**Q: Does the app contain inappropriate language?**
**A: No**
- No profanity
- No offensive language
- Clean, professional tone

### 4. Controlled Substances
**Q: Does the app contain references to controlled substances?**
**A: Yes (Minimal)**
- App references cigarettes/tobacco
- Purpose is smoking cessation (positive health outcome)
- No promotion of substance use
- Educational/informational context only

### 5. User Interaction
**Q: Does the app allow user interaction?**
**A: No**
- No chat features
- No user profiles
- No social sharing built-in
- No user-generated content

### 6. Digital Purchases
**Q: Does the app contain digital purchases?**
**A: No**
- Completely free app
- No in-app purchases
- No subscriptions
- No premium unlock required

### 7. Location Sharing
**Q: Does the app share user location?**
**A: Optional**
- Location is optional for thermal heatmap
- User must explicitly grant permission
- Can be used without location
- Location data stays on device

### 8. Personal Information
**Q: Does the app collect personal information?**
**A: No**
- No name collection
- No email collection
- No phone number
- No account creation
- No analytics tracking (PostHog is optional)

### 9. Advertising
**Q: Does the app contain advertising?**
**A: No**
- No banner ads
- No interstitial ads
- No native ads
- No ad SDKs

### 10. Medical Content
**Q: Does the app provide medical advice?**
**A: No**
- Health milestones are factual, not advice
- No diagnosis features
- No treatment recommendations
- No medical claims

## Age Rating Recommendation

**Category:** Health & Fitness

**Content Descriptors:**
- References to tobacco/smoking (for cessation purposes)
- User location (optional, for heatmap feature)

**Interactive Elements:**
- None

**Supported Age Ranges:**
- Ages 13+
- Ages 16+
- Ages 18+

**Recommended Rating:** PEGI 3+ / Everyone / IARC 3+

**Rationale:**
- App promotes healthy behavior (smoking cessation)
- No harmful content
- No inappropriate material
- Suitable for all ages trying to quit smoking
- No objectionable content

## Target Audience

**Primary Audience:**
- Adults (18-65) trying to quit smoking
- Health-conscious individuals
- People reducing cigarette intake

**Secondary Audience:**

**Not Designed For:**
