## Behavioral Characteristics

### App Usage Patterns
- **Daily active users:** Check app 3-5 times/day
- **Peak usage times:** Morning, lunch, evening (craving times)
- **Session duration:** 30 seconds - 2 minutes
- **Feature usage:**
  - 90% use tracking
  - 70% view charts
  - 50% use Ride It Out
  - 40% set goals
  - 30% backup data

### Motivation Factors
1. **Health improvement** - Primary motivator
2. **Financial savings** - Strong secondary
3. **Social pressure** - Family/friends support
4. **Fear of disease** - Long-term health concerns
5. **Personal achievement** - Streak tracking

---

## Pain Points & Solutions

### Common Frustrations
1. **"I don't know how much I smoke"**
   - Solution: Detailed tracking with timestamps

2. **"I can't see my progress"**
   - Solution: Beautiful charts and analytics

3. **"I give up after a few days"**
   - Solution: Streak system, shields, milestones

4. **"I don't know what triggers me"**
   - Solution: Trigger tagging and pattern analysis

5. **"It's too expensive to quit"**
   - Solution: Cost tracking, money saved display

6. **"I lose my data when I change phones"**
   - Solution: Cloud backup via Google Drive/iCloud
