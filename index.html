Privacy Policy

100% On-Device & Private

🔒 Zero Cloud Tracking Guarantee

All your personal logs, timestamps, notes, and habits stay exclusively on your local device. We never sell, transmit, or monetize your health data.

1. What We Store Locally

  • Smoking logs, timestamps, and craving triggers
  • App configuration, daily limits, pack price, and goals
  • Resisted cravings history and shield awards

2. Your Data Sovereignty

  • Export: Download your entire dataset as JSON/CSV anytime in Settings.
  • Delete: Wipe all records permanently via Clear Data in Settings.

3. Anonymous Telemetry

We use PostHog strictly for anonymous crash reporting and performance metrics. No personally identifiable or health information is ever collected.
